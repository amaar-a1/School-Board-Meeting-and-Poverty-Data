Overview HB0191 (Wyoming, 2026) would require school district board of trustees meetings to be broadcast live over the internet starting July 1, 2026. It also treats broadcast recordings and related meeting records as public records, and it includes a publication requirement for certain warrants. The bill aims to enhance transparency and public access to school governance proceedings.
