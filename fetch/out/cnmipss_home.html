A Message from Jacqueline P. Che
Interim Commissioner of Education,
June 2026 - PRESENT

Dear Valued Stakeholders,

It is with great pleasure and anticipation that I extend a warm welcome to all stakeholders of the CNMI Public School System.

PSS is more than our schools. It is a community of students, families, educators, and staff who show up each day with care, resilience, and hope for what is possible. It is a privilege to serve alongside people who believe deeply in the power of education to shape lives and strengthen our islands.

Head Start/Early Head Start

The Head Start / Early Head Start (CNMI PSS ES/EHS) Program operates on the islands of Saipan, Rota, and Tinian, with the administrative offices located on Saipan.
More Info

Special Education Program

Special Education, as defined by the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act, (IDEA), is specially designed instruction, at no cost to the parents, to meet the unique needs of children with disabilities.
More Info

Early Intervention Program

In 1986, the Individuals with Disabilities Education act was amended to include Early Intervention Services for Infants and Toddlers with disabilities and their families, otherwise known as Part C of IDEA.
