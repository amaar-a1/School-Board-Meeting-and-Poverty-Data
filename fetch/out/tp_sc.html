What Act 28 actually requires

Act 28 of 2025 (S.77) requires all public school boards to livestream board meetings subject to FOIA — full board meetings and committee meetings alike:

  • If the body cannot provide or restore the livestream during a meeting, it must post a clear audio and video recording on its website within two business days.
  • The law explicitly allows services like YouTube, Vimeo, or Facebook Live, as long as links are posted on the district website.
  • Implementation was due by July 1, 2025; local policies based on the State Board's model policy by November 5, 2025.

This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice; the bill text is one click away above.

The two-business-day scramble

Streams fail. The connection drops, the wrong account is signed in, the meeting runs and nobody notices the broadcast didn't. When that happens, the law starts a clock: a clear audio and video recording, on the website, within two business days. That deadline shouldn't depend on one person's afternoon — the person who knows the Zoom login, has time to download a multi-gigabyte file, and remembers where it goes.

A complete recording archive on the district's channel is the belt to the livestream's suspenders. With Teleport, every recording was going up anyway — the failure just stops mattering. Whether that satisfies the statute in your case is for your attorney; Teleport handles the posting step.
