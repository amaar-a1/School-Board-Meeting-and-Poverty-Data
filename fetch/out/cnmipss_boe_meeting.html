19th CNMI State Board of Education - ISA Committee Meeting

  • EVENT
  • Event Date: 10/23/2026 09:00 AM
For your information, the 19th CNMI State Board of Education Standing Committee on Instructional Services & Assessment (ISA) has a meeting scheduled for tomorrow, Friday, October 23, 2026, at 9:00 a.m.
 
Zoom Meeting Link: https://zoom.us/j/97949812765?pwd=xu4kuQYLf5OAm3ABMZ57tThaR7y3jI.1
Meeting ID: 979 4981 2765
Passcode: 819013
 
Pursuant to BOE Policy 0415, public comments are limited to 30 minutes. We ask that remarks be limited to 3 minutes each. Everyone is welcome to submit written comments.
 
Pursuant to 1 CMC § 9912, the Committee may vote to meet in executive session at any time.
