Geography:Newfoundland and Labrador
Account:Education, Literacy, Skills and Training
Information:Enrolment, 1990-2026
Years: 20-21 to 25-26
Data Source:Compiled by the Community Accounts Unit based on information provided by the Department of Education and Early Childhood Development, K-12 School Profile System.
Copyright:Newfoundland & Labrador Statistics Agency, Government of Newfoundland and Labrador

Figures may not add to total due to rounding. For more information see our Frequently Asked Questions.

School District numbers will match those numbers produced by the Department of Education and Early Childhood Development. Beginning in 2003-04, figures do not include private, first nations and other schools located in the geographic area.  As a result, for the Province and all regions (areas including more than one school), numbers will match those published by the Department for 2003-04 to the latest year. Prior to 2003-04, private, first nations and other schools are included. As a result, these numbers may be slightly higher than those published by the Department of Education and Early Childhood Development.

Some schools in Labrador report Grade 3, 4, 5, and 6 students as Grade 5 students and report Grade 7, 8, and 9 students as Grade 8 students in some years. Among the schools are Nukum Mani Shan School and Peenamin McKenzie School (Sheshatshiu Innu School). This will affect total students shown for these grades for higher levels of geography as well.

For some schools, figures may differ considerably over time due to changes in both service areas and grades offered, particularly as a result of the new Schools Act (January 3, 1997).

A number of schools are joint service schools in some years (includes students reported as part of the Roman Catholic school board prior to 1996-97 or reported as part of the francophone school board after 1997-98). Among these are Our Lady Queen of Peace and Elwood Primary School.

A number of schools include additional Grade 10 to Grade 12 students from 1986-87 to 1992-93.  These increases are the result of adults returning to complete high school.

Several private and First Nations schools did not report enrolment numbers between 2011-12 and 2013-14. 

Mud Lake School (K-12) is officially closed as of June 2025 due to zero enrolment.

Leading Tickles Primary, St. Peter’s All Grade (K-12) McCallum and Cabot Academy, Western Bay (K-6) are inactive for 2025-2026 school year. No decision has been made to officially close these schools permanently.

For information on our data handling processes, download the Newfoundland and Labrador Statistics Agency Guidelines for Handling Data (PDF).
