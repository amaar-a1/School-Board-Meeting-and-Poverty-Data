SB99 expands Maryland's Open Meetings Act requirements for county boards of education and the Baltimore City Board of School Commissioners. The bill requires these boards to post open-meeting agendas, approved minutes, and live video streams of open-session portions of meetings on their websites, with archived video retained for five years.
