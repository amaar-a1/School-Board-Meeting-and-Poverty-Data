CHAPTER 3 MEETINGS AND MINUTES A school board or AEA board is a state agency as well as a local agency. Its members are elected by the voters of the local school district, or by the boards of the local school district in the case of an AEA, but it operates within a sphere of legal duties, authorizations and limitations which are prescribed by the state legislature.
