R277-101. Public Participation in Utah State Board of Education Meetings

schools.utah.gov/adminrules/R277-101
R277-101-4. Electronic Meetings. (1) The Board may conduct electronic meetings in accordance with the requirements set forth in Subsection 52-4-207 (3). (2) (a) The Board may allow a member of the Board or member of the public to participate in a Board meeting electronically consistent with available equipment capability.
