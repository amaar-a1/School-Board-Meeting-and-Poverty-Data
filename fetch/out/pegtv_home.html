Public 1075

Channel 1075 is PEGTV’s Public Channel. Public content on PEGTV refers to programming created by community members that reflects local interests, events, and cultural expressions. This content is designed to engage and inform the community and can include a wide range of topics. PEGTV airs local festivals, parades, and celebrations that highlight the community’s culture and activities. As well as in studio talking shows and cooking shows. Channel 1075 also features a Community Bulletin Board which showcases local non-profit events.

Watch Now

Education 1095

The Education Channel aims to provide a platform for various educational content. Programming includes School Board meetings, presentations, and discussions on various topics relevant to students, parents, and educators. In addition, PETGV provides updates on school events, sporting events, important announcements, and information about new initiatives or programs. Channel 1095 also features a Community  Bulletin Board which showcases school news and information from around the region during program breaks.

Watch Now

Government 1085

Channel 1085 is PEGTV’s Government Channel, and provides a platform for local government to communicate directly with residents, showcasing meetings, announcements, and community events. It fosters transparency and encourages civic engagement by allowing citizens to stay informed about local issues and participate in discussions. Additionally, the channel often features programming produced by residents, giving a voice to diverse community perspectives. Channel 1085 also features a Community  Bulletin Board which showcases school news and information from around the region during program breaks.
