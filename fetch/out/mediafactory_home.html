Welcome to the Media Factory

We're a community of media makers who work in tv, film, radio, and more. At the Media Factory, we provide the tools, training, space and access to distribution that give you the power to share your story.

Learn more about who we are.

Morning Announcements

Morning Announcements is a weekly highlight of the happenings at the Media Factory, hosted by the dedicated staff. We’ll share what’s happening each week, talk with local producers and creators, and play some excellent and eclectic music.

 

Listen to all episodes of Morning Announcements and more on the 99.3FM WBTV-LP Mixcloud
