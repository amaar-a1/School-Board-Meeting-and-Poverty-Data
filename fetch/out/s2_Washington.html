RCW 42.30.220 - Washington

app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/default.aspx?cite=42.30.220
PDF RCW 42.30.220 Recording or online streaming by agency encouraged. (1) Public agencies are encouraged to make an audio or video recording of, or to provide an online streaming option for, all regular meetings of its governing body, and to make recordings of these meetings available online for a minimum of six months.

Washington School Board Meeting Recordings: RCW 42.30.035, Posted ...

www.teleport-app.com/washington-school-board-meeting-recordings
Does Washington require posting meeting recordings online? For most meetings, no. Under RCW 42.30.220 the legislature encourages public agencies to record their regular meetings and make the recordings available online for at least six months — but it does not require it. The hard requirements for school boards are recording, one-year retention, and providing copies electronically on request.
