What § 551.128 actually requires

The Texas Open Meetings Act (Government Code Chapter 551) requires home-rule cities with a population of 50,000 or more, counties of 125,000 or more, and school districts with 10,000 or more students to:

  • Make video and audio recordings of regularly scheduled open meetings.
  • Post the archived recording on the internet within 7 days.
  • Keep it available online for at least 2 years.

The baseline for every Texas governmental body is § 551.021: minutes or a recording of each open meeting is mandatory. This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice; the statute text is one click away above.

Frequently asked questions

What does Texas Government Code § 551.128 require?

Home-rule cities with a population of 50,000 or more, counties of 125,000 or more, and school districts with 10,000 or more students must make video and audio recordings of regularly scheduled open meetings, post the archived recording on the internet within 7 days, and keep it available online for at least 2 years.

We are under the population threshold. Does any of this apply to us?

The § 551.128 posting requirement applies above the thresholds. But § 551.021 requires every Texas governmental body to keep minutes or a recording of each open meeting — and residents expect the video online either way. Whether a rule applies to your body is a question for your attorney.

Does posting to YouTube count as posting on the internet?

The statute requires the archived recording to be available on the internet, and many Texas bodies publish to a YouTube channel linked from their website. Whether your specific setup satisfies the statute is a question for your attorney — this page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.
