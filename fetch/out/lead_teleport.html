Frequently asked questions

Does posting to YouTube satisfy these posting rules?

Usually the statute doesn't prescribe a platform — it says post the recording online, on or linked from your website. Many bodies use YouTube for exactly that. Whether your setup satisfies your state's rule is a question for your attorney; each state page links the actual statute so you can check the text.

My state isn't listed. Does that mean nothing is required?

Most states require minutes only — but check your statute, and check it again next session; this list has grown every year.
