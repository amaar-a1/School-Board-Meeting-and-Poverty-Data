Indiana's meeting livestream law: who must stream, what you must keep, and for how long. Since July 1, 2025, Indiana's primary local governing bodies must live stream their public meetings, archive the video with links to the agenda, minutes, and memoranda, put the URL in the meeting notice, and keep the recording at least 90 days. One year in, the first complaints have already arrived.
