What HEA 1167 actually requires

Indiana's HEA 1167, effective July 1, 2025, requires county boards of commissioners, town councils, and school boards to:

  • Livestream their public meetings.
  • Archive the recordings on their website for at least 90 days.

Bodies without internet capability may instead record the meeting and post the recording. This page is a plain-language summary, not legal advice; see the analyses at fbtgibbons.com and kgrlaw.com for the details.

Where compliance actually breaks

The livestream is the visible part, and YouTube Live handles it free. The recurring work is the archive: after every meeting, a multi-gigabyte recording has to come out of Zoom, go up online, get titled, and get linked — and then somebody has to make sure it stays there for 90 days. That job lands on a clerk or an auditor's office, meeting after meeting, with no one hired to do it.
