The 10,000-student rule

Texas law requires school districts with a student enrollment of 10,000 or more to make their board meetings available online (Texas Government Code § 551.128). That covers the Big, Large and Huge tiers below.

Overall

Meetings per 10,000 students = total available meetings ÷ total enrollment × 10,000. Meetings per poverty point = average meetings ÷ average child poverty rate (in percentage points).
