      Map of school districts

        Loading district borders…

        Hover over or tap a district to see its figures; click to zoom in. District borders are the Census Bureau's 2024 cartographic boundaries (simplified). Unified and elementary districts are shown; in states that also have separate high school districts, tick “Secondary districts” to draw those on top. Guam, the Northern Mariana Islands and American Samoa have one territory-wide district each, so their outline is the whole territory. Vermont's poverty figures are for supervisory unions, so each Vermont town district shows its union's figures; districts merged in 2024 show the merged district's figures on their old borders. High school pieces of districts show their parent district. Districts the Census poverty file leaves out (Defense Department base schools, districts with no schools of their own, a few tiny districts) show their county's all-ages poverty rate, noted in their tooltip. Districts with no meetings found count as 0 (palest blue). Pale green areas are outside any school district, mostly national parks and other federal land. When a state is picked or filters are on, other districts are faded.
